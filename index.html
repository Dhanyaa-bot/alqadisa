OUR STORY

Al-Qadisa Institute is located in Bantwal Taluk of Dakshina Kannada District, Karnataka. The academy is set in a beautiful 25-acre area in Bengathodu, Kavalkatte , which is about 15 km from BC Road - Belthangady Road.

Dr. Hazrath Muhammad Fazil Razvi, popularly known as Kavalkatte Hazrath, leads the academy alongside a dedicated team of scholars and distinguished individuals.
