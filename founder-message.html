A Legacy of Learning, A Future of Faithful Leadership

Founder's Message

The founder of Al-Qadisa Institute, Dr. Muhammad Fazil Razvi Hazrath, hails from Channarayapatna in the Hassan district. He completed his schooling in his village and obtained his Fazil degree from Darul Uloom Shahe Jamaat Sharia College, Hassan, in 1993. After earning his Fazil degree, Hazrath began serving as a Khateeb at the Hanafi Juma Masjid in Kavalkatte . During this period, he mastered the Tibbunnabavi method of treatment and also practiced Manevaidaya and Unani medicine. Addition-ally, he gained expertise in spiritual healing methods through Quranic verses, Zikr, and Asmaas.

After a decade of service at Kavalkatte Masjid, Hazrath began guiding people who sought solutions to various issues, including illnesses. As the number of people seeking remedies for their problems increased, it became difficult to accommodate them within the mosque's services. To address this, he established a separate system nearby, providing guidance and shelter to thousands from different parts of the state.

True service is not limited to resolving physical, mental, familial, or business-related issues; it is only complete when a community is granted meaningful access to spiri-tuality and education. Hazrath recognized that education and spirituality must go hand in hand to free society from harmful influences. He believed that only by prepar-ing enlightened individuals could the community ensure a brighter future for the next generation. In 2015, Hazrath established Al-Qadisa Institute, an institution dedicat-ed to educational excellence. With the vision of acquiring 25 acres of land for the institution, he approached donors who were deeply concerned about the communi-ty's progress. Their overwhelming support enabled Al-Qadisa to swiftly secure the necessary land, ensuring a strong foundation for the institution's mission.
