Al-Qadisa Pu College

Al-Qadisa Institute PU College (Boys’ Section) was established in 2024-25 after receiving recognition from the Government of Karnataka. The Girls’ Section is set to commence in 2025-26 to meet the educational needs of the local community, incorporating Sharia-based classes. Al-Qadisa Institute PU College is committed to creating a conducive learning environment while safeguarding students from the negative influences prevalent in many college campuses today. The institution remains dedicated to imparting knowledge and spirituality in line with its educational mission.
